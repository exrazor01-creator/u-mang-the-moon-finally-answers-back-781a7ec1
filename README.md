The Moon Finally Answers Back — one line in the voice of the Moon replying at last, judged two at a time.

## The entry

> Four billion years of listening to wishes, and nobody has ever once wished me a good night back.

96 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–240 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **Speaks as the Moon** — an answer to the wishes of everyone staring up at it, from a body that has been on duty for four billion years.
2. **Funny on one read** — a real laugh, not a polite smile: the Moon only wants the courtesy returned.
3. **The Moon's own voice** — wry and old and a little tired of being wished upon.
4. **Lands as a comeback** — the turn arrives in the last words, "wished me a good night back", before the line ends.
5. **Wastes no characters** — 96 of 240, every word earning its place, and it never explains its own joke.
